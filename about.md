# About IMAP

The Interstellar Mapping and Acceleration Probe (IMAP) explores our solar neighborhood and decodes the messages in particles from the Sun and beyond our cosmic shield. Learn more about IMAP: [Interstellar Mapping and Acceleration Probe (IMAP) mission](https://imap.princeton.edu).

## I-ALiRT: Low Latency Space Weather Data

Space weather data can be accessed through IMAP's low-latency I-ALiRT system. See the [Space Weather](https://imap-mission.com/ialirt) pages for current plots and more information on the I-ALiRT [data access API](https://imap-mission.com/ialirt/api).
